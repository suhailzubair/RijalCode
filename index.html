A few things I need you to know

You are already enough.

My love, happy birthday. I pray that Allah blesses you with a life filled with happiness, peace, good health, success, and countless beautiful moments. May He protect your heart from sadness, wipe away every worry you carry silently, and give you reasons to smile even on the days when smiling feels difficult. May Allah increase you in faith, wisdom, beauty, and everything good, and may He make every dream you have come true in ways even better than you imagined.

You have become someone whose happiness genuinely matters to me. When you're happy, I feel happy. When you're hurting, I wish I could take the pain away from you. When you are tired, I wish I could be there to make things easier. And when you doubt yourself, I hope you remember that there is someone who sees so much beauty and goodness in you, even when you cannot see it yourself.

I love the little things about you: the way you talk, the way you laugh, the way you get angry, the way you express yourself, the random things you say, and the moments when you don't even realize how adorable you are.

I love the version of you that everyone sees, but I also love the quiet you, the vulnerable you, the tired you, the imperfect you — the you that doesn't always have everything figured out. You don't have to be perfect for me to appreciate you. You are already enough.

If I could give you one thing…

I would give you the ability to see yourself through my eyes for just one day. Then maybe you'd understand why I smile whenever I think about you; why your presence means so much to me; why, among all the people in this enormous world, my heart chose you.

If Allah allows me, I want to make beautiful memories with you. I want to watch you grow, succeed, become everything you've dreamed of becoming, and be there to celebrate you through every chapter of your life. I want to be there for the ordinary days, not just the special ones.

My birthday prayer for you

May Allah write happiness into every chapter of your life.

May He give you a heart that remains peaceful even when life becomes difficult. May He surround you with people who genuinely love and value you, protect you from people who don't deserve your heart, and open doors you never expected to open.

May He turn your tears into smiles, your worries into ease, and your prayers into beautiful answers. And may He grant you a future so beautiful that one day you'll look back at today and realize that every difficult moment was worth surviving.

And if Allah writes me into that future with you, I will consider it one of the greatest blessings of my life.

For now and always

You deserve to be loved gently, sincerely, and completely.

Thank you for existing. Thank you for being you. Thank you for every smile you've given me, every conversation we've had, every memory we've created, and every little moment that has made my life brighter.

Somewhere, at this exact moment, there is someone smiling because you were born. Someone was grateful for you. Someone was praying for you. Someone was loving you.

And that someone is me. ❤️
